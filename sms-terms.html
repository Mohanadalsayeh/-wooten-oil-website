Program description

Customers who opt in may receive low-volume transactional text messages from Wooten Oil Co Inc. Messages may include fuel-request confirmations, delivery updates, account notifications, verification codes, and customer-support replies. The program is not intended for promotional marketing.

Consent

Enrollment requires your affirmative consent through the Wooten Oil text message sign-up form. The consent checkbox is not selected automatically. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies according to your account activity, requests, and support interactions. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless carrier plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. After opting out, you may receive one confirmation message. Reply HELP for help, email Support@wootenoil.com, or call (901) 476-2684. To enroll again after opting out, submit the sign-up form again or follow the instructions provided by Wooten Oil.

Delivery

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to network availability and carrier policies.

Privacy

Your participation is governed by the Wooten Oil Privacy Policy. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
