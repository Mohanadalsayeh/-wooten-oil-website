Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Wooten Oil Co Inc. respects the privacy of customers and website visitors. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information provided through our website, customer portal, service forms, and text messaging program.

Information we collect

We may collect contact and account information such as your name, address, email address, telephone number, customer number, service requests, payment-related account records, and communications with Wooten Oil. Our systems may also collect technical information needed for security and operation, including IP address, browser type, and access time.

How information is used

We use information to provide petroleum delivery and customer services, operate and secure the customer portal, respond to requests, maintain account records, send requested transactional notifications, and comply with legal obligations.

Account applications and identity documents

When you request to open an account, we may collect business or personal contact information and copies of a Tax ID document, driver’s license, or state identification card. We use this information only to evaluate and administer the requested Wooten Oil account, verify identity, prevent fraud, contact the applicant, and meet legal or recordkeeping requirements. Uploaded application documents are stored privately and are restricted to authorized Wooten Oil administrators and service providers needed to operate secure storage.

Text messaging privacy

If you separately opt in, Wooten Oil may send transactional text messages such as fuel-request confirmations, delivery updates, account notifications, verification codes, and customer-support replies. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help deliver the messaging program and are required to protect the information, or when disclosure is required by law.

Sharing and service providers

We may use service providers to host the website, operate communications, process authorized transactions, secure systems, and support business operations. We do not sell personal information. Service providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform services for Wooten Oil.

Choices and retention

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. We retain information only as reasonably necessary for business, security, compliance, and recordkeeping purposes.
