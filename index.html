About Us

A long history of local petroleum service.

Wooten Oil specializes in the wholesale distribution and local supply of petroleum products, including gasoline and diesel fuel for commercial and municipal needs throughout West Tennessee.

Established in 1939Founded as a regional fuel and petroleum supplier.
Local LeadershipGuided for more than 50 years by William Henry Wooten, a prominent Tipton County businessman.
Petroleum DistributionWholesale gasoline and diesel supply for commercial and municipal customers.
West Tennessee ServiceA traditional bulk fuel jobber supporting the region's energy and transportation needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can we help?

Quick answers about fuel delivery, farm fuel, Oil Cards, and Wooten Oil locations.

What types of fuel does Wooten Oil deliver?
Wooten Oil serves local petroleum needs including gasoline, on-road diesel, and farm/off-road diesel. For a specific product or delivery requirement, contact us to confirm availability.
How do I request a fuel delivery?
Open the Request Fuel Delivery page, or call Wooten Oil at (901) 476-2684.
Do you deliver farm and off-road diesel?
Yes. Wooten Oil provides farm/off-road diesel service for qualifying farm, equipment, and commercial needs. Contact us for pricing and delivery availability.
What areas do you deliver to?
Delivery availability depends on the location and fuel requirement. Send a fuel request with your delivery address or call us at (901) 476-2684 to confirm service for your area.
Where can I use my Wooten Oil Card?
Your Wooten Oil Card can be used at the participating locations shown in the Oil Card and Locations sections of this website.
How do I get help with a Wooten Oil Card?
Call Wooten Oil at (901) 476-2684 for Oil Card questions and account assistance.
How can commercial customers contact Wooten Oil?
Call (901) 476-2684 or email Support@wootenoil.com to discuss commercial fuel needs.

Need fuel delivered?

Contact Wooten Oil for pricing, availability, and delivery options for your farm, business, equipment, or gas station.
