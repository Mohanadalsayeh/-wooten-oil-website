WOOTEN OIL — STATEMENTS & INVOICES ADMIN UPDATE

The Statements & Invoices admin tab now contains two sub-tabs:
1. Customer Documents
2. Send Account Statements

Customer Documents now has these Sending Options:
- Customer Portal
- Email PDF
- SMS secure PDF link

Behavior
- The PDF is securely stored as a customer document.
- Customer Portal creates the customer's portal notification.
- Email PDF attaches the uploaded PDF to the customer's email.
- SMS sends the secure login-protected PDF link using Twilio.
- The colored/gray Email, Phone, and Portal selections in Live Customer Data are respected.
- Each upload and its selected delivery results are recorded in Communication History.
- At least one Sending Option must be selected before uploading.
