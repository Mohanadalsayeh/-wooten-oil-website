- PDF errors remain visible with their reason.
- Recent statement runs and Export PDF share an aligned toolbar above the table.
- Export PDF includes every customer in the selected run(s), across table pages.
- Removed the extra overall progress bar at the upper right; customer bars remain.
- Standard portal X close button and bottom-right Done button.
- Done/X close the window without cancelling the statement run.
- Fits desktop and phone screens; the wide customer table scrolls inside the window on phones.
- Applies to manual sends, scheduled runs, Test A, Test B and Test All.
