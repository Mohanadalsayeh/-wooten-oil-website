Notifications

Send Customer Notification

Send a private portal notification to one customer, with optional email and SMS delivery.

Send notification to
This sends the notification to every customer with a valid email address on file. Each matching customer will receive the message by email and in the Wooten Oil customer portal.

Up to 3 files, 5 MB each, 10 MB total. Attachments are available for one-customer notifications. The customer can open them from the notification window and they are also attached to the email.

SMS is available for one-customer notifications. The phone number comes from the MAS 90 customer record.
Send Documents

Customer Documents

Upload secure PDF statements and invoices to a customer's online account. Customers can open them from My Account → Statements & Invoices.

Sending Options Checked boxes are the delivery methods used when this document is uploaded.

Security: Documents are stored privately and can only be opened by the customer account they are assigned to. PDF limit: 10 MB per file.
